# Privacy

Your files are processed locally in your browser whenever possible. We don't upload your files to our
servers.

- No account, email, or login is required to use any tool.
- No analytics or tracking reads or reports on file contents.
- No uploaded files are stored remotely, because for local tools, we never receive them.
- The one exception today is anything explicitly marked "Coming Soon" (e.g. video tools, the URL
  shortener) — those don't work yet at all, so no data is sent anywhere for them either.

See the in-app Privacy page (`/privacy`) for the user-facing version of this document.
